# Personal portfolio

Dark personal index with a 680px reading column. At wide desktop widths (1120px and up), the existing profile and section links sit in a 240px rail beside the reading column; smaller screens keep the single-column layout. Compact 28px name, 80 by 96px color portrait, 15px readable body text and 16px section headings. Existing Inter font, neutral #101112 canvas, #e8e8e6 text, #a1a3a5 secondary text, and #2b2d30 separators.

No site-wide navbar, slogans, emojis, oversized hero, or footer. Mobile uses a 23px name and 64 by 80px portrait. Project, career, research, and achievement rows use thin separators; career certificate links align at the right edge. Motion is limited to the active section underline, a brief content fade, and small link feedback, with reduced-motion support. Links have visible keyboard focus. Original CV facts and publication statuses remain authoritative.
